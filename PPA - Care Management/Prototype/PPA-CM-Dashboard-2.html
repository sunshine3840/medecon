Definitions & Methodology
×
Actively Engaged (Care Management)
A member is considered "Actively Engaged" if they have: (1) Completed initial care management assessment and agreed to participate, (2) Active care plan on file, and (3) At least one documented interaction with a care manager within the last 30 days (phone, in-person, or telehealth).
Source: Care Management Operations Team | Validated: 06/2026
Risk Stratification Methodology
Members are stratified into risk tiers using 3M Clinical Risk Groups (CRGs):
  • High Risk: Top 1% of population by predicted cost and clinical complexity (CRG catastrophic and dominant chronic categories)
  • Moderate Risk: Next 2-5% of population (CRG multiple chronic conditions)
  • Low Risk: Remaining population (CRG healthy, acute, and single chronic condition)
Algorithm: 3M Clinical Risk Groups v2.1 | Claims data through 05/31/2026
Potentially Preventable ER Visits
ER visits flagged as potentially preventable using 3M Potentially Preventable Events (PPE) algorithm. This industry-standard tool identifies ER visits for conditions that could have been managed in an outpatient setting with appropriate access to primary care, chronic disease management, or preventive services.
Algorithm: 3M Potentially Preventable Events (PPE) v3.2 | Validated by peer-reviewed studies
Potentially Preventable Readmissions
30-day readmissions flagged as potentially preventable using 3M Potentially Preventable Readmissions (PPR) algorithm. This tool identifies readmissions that could have been avoided with better discharge planning, medication reconciliation, timely follow-up, or care coordination.
Algorithm: 3M Potentially Preventable Readmissions (PPR) v3.2 | Industry standard for quality measurement
Clinical Drivers (Diagnostic Groupings)
Utilization is categorized by clinical diagnostic groupings using Optum Impact Pro methodology. This groups claims by primary diagnosis into clinically meaningful categories (e.g., CHF, COPD, Diabetes, Asthma) rather than service categories (e.g., inpatient, outpatient), enabling actionable clinical conversations.
Methodology: Optum Impact Pro Clinical Groupings | Updated quarterly
Member ID (Plan Member ID)
All member lists display Plan Member ID as the primary identifier. This ID is used across care management systems (TrueCare, case management platforms) for consistent member matching. If you need to cross-reference with other ID types (Subscriber ID, Member ID), contact the data team for a crosswalk file.
Standard: Plan Member ID | Format: [State]-[Year]-[7-digit number]
Provider Attribution Logic
Members are attributed to the provider (PCP or provider group/TIN) with the plurality of evaluation and management (E&M) visits in the last 12 months. Attribution is refreshed monthly. If a member has no E&M visits in the last 12 months, they are attributed based on the most recent PCP assignment in the enrollment file.
Attribution Date: As of 06/01/2026 | Refresh Frequency: Monthly
Data Freshness & Claims Lag
Claims Data: Processed through 05/31/2026. Typical claims lag is 30-45 days for complete adjudication.
Care Management Data: Updated weekly from TrueCare system. Last refresh: 06/23/2026.
ADT (Discharge) Data: Real-time feed for readmission risk alerts. Discharge data available within 24-48 hours.
Dashboard Refresh Date: 06/24/2026
Unable to Reach Criteria
A member is flagged as "Unable to Reach" if care management has made 3 or more documented outreach attempts (phone, mail, text) over at least 2 weeks with no successful contact. Attempts must include calls at different times of day. Members with invalid contact information (disconnected phone, returned mail) are flagged after 2 attempts.
Source: Care Management Outreach Protocol | Updated: Q2 2026
Industry Benchmarks (Market Average)
Market averages represent the median performance across all provider groups in the same state and line of business (e.g., Illinois Medicaid). Benchmarks are calculated quarterly and exclude outliers (top and bottom 5% of providers by panel size).
Benchmark Period: Q1 2026 (Jan-Mar 2026) | Updated quarterly
